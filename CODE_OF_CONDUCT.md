# Code of conduct

We want Orqara to be a welcoming, productive project.

Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and avoid harassment,
discrimination, threats, sexualized content, or publishing another person's private information.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct harms the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub](https://github.com/HUSAM-07/orqara). Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
